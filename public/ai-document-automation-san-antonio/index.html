The honest version

Three reasons not to hire me for this.

If one of these is true you will not get your money back on it. Better said here than after.

Reason one

It happens a few times a month

Automation pays off on repetition. If a document path runs less than roughly weekly, the build will cost more than the time it saves and you should leave it alone.

Reason two

Every document is different

If no two of your documents share a shape, there is no pattern to automate and you will spend more time correcting the output than doing the work by hand.

Reason three

The output cannot be reviewed

If nobody will check the result, do not automate it. Document errors are silent and they compound. I build review into these workflows and I will not take it out.
