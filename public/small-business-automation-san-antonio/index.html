The problem

Your tools work. The handoffs do not.

Most small businesses already have enough software. The real bottleneck is the space between tools: the phone call that never becomes a CRM note, the quote that never gets a reminder, the spreadsheet that someone updates at night.

The honest version

Three reasons not to hire me for this.

If one of these is true you will not get your money back on it. Better said here than after.

Reason one

It happens a few times a month

Automation pays off on repetition. Under roughly weekly, the build costs more than the time it saves. Tell me what else is eating your week instead.

Reason two

Your process is not settled yet

Automating a workflow you are still changing means rebuilding it every month. Get the process stable by hand first, then automate the version that survived.

Reason three

You want one system that does everything

That is a suite purchase, not a $250 build. If you want reviews, payments, CRM and automation in one dashboard with a support line, buy the suite. I will tell you that for free.

Do I need to pay a monthly retainer for automation?

Not for work at this size. A confirmation text, a reminder sequence, or a lead follow up is a build, not a subscription. You pay once, you own the accounts, and the only recurring cost is the tool itself. Retainers make sense when somebody is actively managing campaigns for you every month, which is not what this is.

What does the $250 build actually include, and what is the $50 a month?

One workflow mapped end to end, built inside accounts that belong to you, tested on real jobs, and handed over with the logins and a walkthrough. Typical builds are booking confirmations, reminder and review request sequences, quote follow ups, and lead form routing. The optional $50 a month is support only, not a licence: skip it and the automation still runs, you just pay hourly if it ever needs work. Cancel any month.
