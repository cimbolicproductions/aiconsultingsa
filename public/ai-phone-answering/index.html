The problem

How much does missed call text back cost for a small business?

The raw carrier cost is $3 to $15 per month. A Twilio phone number is $1.15 per month, each text costs roughly $0.008 plus carrier fees of about $0.003 to $0.0065, and A2P 10DLC registration is $4 one time plus a small monthly campaign fee. Standalone tools charge $20 to $100 per month, AI answering services start around $25 per month, and CRM suites that bundle it run $300 to $500 per month. Do I need a monthly subscription for missed call text back?

No. It is a phone number, a rule that detects an unanswered call, and one outbound text. Subscriptions wrap that in a dashboard and a support desk, which is genuinely worth paying for if you want someone to call when it breaks. If you do not, the same thing runs on your own Twilio account for a few dollars a month. Check with your current phone provider first, because many include it already.

Who owns the phone automation after you build it?

You do. I build it inside a Twilio account in your business name, using your number, and you hold the login. No contract, nothing to cancel. If you stop working with me it keeps running, and any other developer can maintain it because it is standard Twilio and not a proprietary platform.

How is this different from a virtual receptionist service?

A virtual receptionist service is usually a rented script and recurring service. I build a custom Twilio workflow around how your business answers, qualifies, routes, and follows up on calls.

Does the AI phone answering system work in Spanish?

Yes. English and Spanish voice workflows can be configured for San Antonio callers.

What happens when a caller needs a human?

The workflow can transfer urgent or human requested calls to your phone, then still log the call details for review.
