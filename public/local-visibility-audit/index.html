The verdict

You win when a buyer already knows your name. You are hard to find when they do not.

  • Branded search: strong. The business appeared first in the branded query sampled.
  • Generic buyer search: weak. Competitors appeared in the sampled results where RentwashersSA did not.
  • AI discovery: real, but narrow. Customer reports supported discovery through ChatGPT. One test prompt was not treated as proof.

  3. Keep one consistent pricing story.
  4. Keep the branded page that already works.
