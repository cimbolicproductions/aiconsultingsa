Why it works

You do not have to be cheapest. You have to be checkable.

Google hands back ten links and lets the visitor decide, so it never has to be right. An answer engine has to commit. When ChatGPT tells someone a price, it is staking its own credibility on your number.

So it behaves like anyone making a bet. It picks the source least likely to embarrass it. My competitor advertised a number that fell apart under checking, and their own marketing became evidence against them. Mine was $60, and $60 was the whole story.

The model rewards the number it can trust and penalizes the number it has to correct. That is the entire mechanic, and almost nobody local is doing anything about it yet.
